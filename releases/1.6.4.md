# LLM Workbench v1.6.4

## Overview

Two additions after v1.6.3. Every applet has a **Full screen** button that shows it alone on
the whole screen, and a conversation can be **renamed in the sidebar**.

Four commits on `dev` since v1.6.3 (2026-11-21), 10 files, +317 / −13.

---

## ⛶ Full screen for applets

A **Full screen** button sits after Troubleshoot under every chart, dashboard or other applet.

- **A second copy, built for the screen.** The copy in the chat is sized for the chat: its
  frame is as tall as its content and its charts are capped to the chat area. Full screen
  builds a new copy whose charts and images are capped to 92% of the screen height and which
  scrolls inside its own frame when it is taller.
- **It starts over.** The copy is loaded again, so it fetches fresh data. The one in the chat
  is untouched.
- **Small applets are enlarged.** A clock, a gauge or a single card that is narrower than 90%
  of the screen and shorter than it is enlarged up to 4× and centred. If enlarging makes the
  content re-wrap and overflow, the enlargement backs off until it fits. Content that already
  spans the width (responsive charts, dashboards) keeps its size.
- **Leaving.** Esc, the ✕ in the top right corner, or leaving the browser's fullscreen mode
  returns to the chat.
- **If the browser refuses fullscreen**, the applet still covers the whole page.

## ✎ Rename a conversation

Double-click a title in the sidebar, or hover over the row and click the pencil (✎), and type
the new title in place.

- **Enter or clicking away saves, Escape cancels.** An empty title keeps the old one.
- **A rename is kept.** The automatic title is only written while a chat is still called
  "New conversation".
- **The list holds still while you type.** The sidebar used to be redrawn whenever a turn
  finished in any conversation; that redraw now waits until the edit ends.
- **Server check.** `PATCH /api/conversations/:id` flattens the title to one line, trims it
  and cuts it to 120 characters, the same limit as automatic titles. An empty title is
  answered with 400; before, the route stored whatever it received.
- **Title filter.** If the "Filter by title…" box is in use and the new title no longer
  matches it, the chat leaves the filtered list until the box is cleared.
- **Obsidian.** The next Save to Obsidian renames the note to match, as it already did for
  automatic title changes.

## ✅ Validation

`npm test`: 186/186, two more than v1.6.3, both for the title check.

The title route was exercised in a throwaway process: a padded two-line title came back as
one trimmed line, a 300-character one as 120 characters, blank and missing titles as 400, an
unknown conversation as 404. **The rename control itself was not tried in a browser before
this release.**

Full screen has no automated tests; it is browser code. One measurement is on record from
2026-11-21: a 510px digital watch enlarged 3.01× re-wrapped its digits and overflowed the
screen, and settles at 2.83× with the padding term and the back-off.

The system prompt is unchanged from v1.6.3. No `.env` change, no extension change.
**Upgrade:** `git pull` → restart → hard-reload the page.
