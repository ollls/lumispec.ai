# LLM Workbench v1.5.7

## Overview

A running answer survives leaving its conversation. Switching to another conversation used to
abort the turn in flight — answer, tool results and reasoning gone, an empty assistant message
left behind — and starting a new chat let the old stream reset the new one's controls and wipe
its bubble. Conversations now run side by side, each on its own slot.

Also in this release: charts that fit the chat area, and a prompt box that keeps the height you
dragged it to.

Six commits on `dev` since v1.5.6 (2026-11-08), 13 files, +716 / −113.

---

## 🔀 Conversations that keep running

- **A turn belongs to its conversation, not to the screen.** Switch away or start a new chat and
  the turn keeps going; come back and its live bubble is there, still filling, with Stop shown and
  the timer counting from when the turn started.
- **Controls follow what is on screen.** Send, Stop, the elapsed timer, the context bar and
  scrolling belong to the visible conversation. Stop stops that conversation only.
- **Approvals go to the right place.** Approve / Deny and pipeline Continue / Retry are sent to the
  conversation that asked; Enter approves only while that conversation is on screen.
- **A pulsing dot** in the sidebar marks every conversation that is working. Deleting a
  conversation stops its turn.
- **Limit:** reloading the page still closes the connections and stops every running turn.

Parallel turns share the GPU, so each one generates more slowly.

## ⏹ Stopped turns keep their work

- A stopped chat turn is stored as `[Stopped before an answer was written.]` together with its
  reasoning and every tool result gathered so far. The placeholder used to stay empty, and that
  empty assistant turn was replayed in the history of later requests.
- A stopped pipeline keeps its completed tasks and records the task that was running as
  "Stopped before this task finished."

## 📐 Applets fit the chat area

"Draw SPY chart" produced a canvas with `height="340"` and `responsive: true`; Chart.js read the
canvas's own 300×340 as the aspect ratio and rendered the chart 1,763px tall in a 625px chat area.

- `applet-fit.js` computes a cap from the visible chat area (80%, clamped to 240–1200px). SVGs and
  images get a max-height; a Chart.js plugin wraps an oversized chart in a fixed-height box before
  its first resize. Charts that already fit are untouched.
- The chartjs prompt section now shows the wrapper + `maintainAspectRatio: false` pattern and
  forbids width/height attributes on the canvas.

## ↕ Prompt box keeps its dragged height

In a prompt pane dragged taller than ~230px, the first keystroke or paste pinned the textarea to
200px. Auto-sizing now leaves the textarea filling the pane when the pane has a user-set height.

## ✅ Validation

`npm test`: 119/119 (112 at v1.5.6 + 7, `test/appletFit.test.js`). The concurrent-conversation
changes were exercised in Chrome against a local model: two conversations answering at once,
leaving and returning to a running turn, and Stop after a tool call. Pipeline runs in the
background and approval prompts across a switch were not exercised.

No `.env` change, no extension change. **Upgrade:** `git pull` → restart → hard-reload the page.
