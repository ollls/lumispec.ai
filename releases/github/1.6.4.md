# LLM Workbench: lumispec.ai, v1.6.4

Two additions after v1.6.3.

- **Full screen for applets** — a button under every chart, dashboard or other applet shows it alone on the whole screen. It is a second copy sized for the screen and loaded again, so it fetches fresh data; a small applet such as a clock is enlarged up to 4× and centred. Esc or the ✕ returns to the chat.
- **Rename a conversation** — double-click a title in the sidebar, or click the pencil that appears on hover. Enter saves, Escape cancels. A renamed chat keeps its title, and the list is not redrawn while you type.
- **Title check on the server** — titles are flattened to one line and cut to 120 characters; an empty one is refused.

`npm test`: 186/186. No `.env` change, no extension change, system prompt unchanged. Renaming was tried by hand in the browser, by double-click and by the pencil. **Upgrade:** `git pull` → restart → hard-reload the page.
